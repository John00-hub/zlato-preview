Sector Focus

The Oil & Gas Desk

Energy work demands lawyers who speak the industry's language. Zlato Alliance advises participants at every point of the value chain:

  • Licensing rounds, asset acquisitions and farm-in / farm-out agreements
  • Petroleum Industry Act 2021 — transition, structuring and compliance
  • Nigerian Content (NOGICD Act) planning and documentation
  • Drilling, service and supply contracts; JOAs and PSCs
  • Energy disputes, decommissioning and host-community frameworks
